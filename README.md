# 💉 How to Anaesthetise...

> *a totally serious clinical decision support tool*
> *(it is not. please don't.)*

## What is this?

A state-of-the-art, evidence-free, vibe-driven pre-operative assessment app. You fill in **everything** about the patient, then press one big button and receive the only answer that matters.

## Features

- 🧍 Patient details (age, gender, height, weight)
- 🔪 Operation speciality and the operation itself
- ⏱️ Operation length (surgeon's estimate, ×2 for reality)
- 🫀 Medical history, 💊 drug history, 🤧 allergies
- 👪 Family history (includes "uncle woke up mid-surgery")
- 🍻 Social history (no judgement, some judgement)
- ✨ Bonus round: star sign, surgeon mood, anaesthetist caffeine level
- 🌈 One button that runs the **proprietary Vibes Engine™**

## How to use

1. Open `index.html`
2. Fill in all the boxes with great seriousness
3. Press **"How do I anaesthetise this patient?"**
4. Receive vibes
5. Immediately call your consultant

## FAQ

**Does it calculate drug doses?**
No. It calculates *aura*.

**Can I use this on a real patient?**
Absolutely not. Please use real drugs, real monitoring, a real airway plan and a real senior who has had coffee.

**The output just says "vibes".**
Yes. That is the output.

**Is the patient fine?**
They said "I'm fine" six times. They are not fine.
